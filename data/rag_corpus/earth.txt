Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only planet known to support life. It has a diameter of 12,742 kilometers and orbits at an average distance of 149.6 million kilometers, which is defined as one astronomical unit.

Earth rotates once every 23.9 hours and completes one orbit in 365.25 days. It has one natural moon. About 71 percent of the surface is covered by oceans.

The atmosphere of Earth is mostly nitrogen, at about 78 percent, with about 21 percent oxygen and small amounts of other gases. The average surface temperature is roughly 15 degrees Celsius. Liquid water is stable on the surface, which is essential for life as we know it.
