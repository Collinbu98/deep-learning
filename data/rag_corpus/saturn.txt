Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is famous for its bright ring system. It has a diameter of 116,460 kilometers and orbits at an average distance of 1.43 billion kilometers.

Saturn rotates quickly. A day on Saturn lasts about 10.7 hours, and a year lasts about 29.4 Earth years. Saturn is the least dense planet, with an average density of 0.687 grams per cubic centimeter, which is less than the density of water.

The rings of Saturn are made mostly of water ice, along with some rock and dust. Saturn has 146 known moons. Its largest moon, Titan, has a thick atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane and ethane.
