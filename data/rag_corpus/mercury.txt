Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It has a diameter of 4,879 kilometers and orbits the Sun at an average distance of 57.9 million kilometers. Mercury is the smallest of the eight planets.

Mercury spins slowly on its axis. One day on Mercury lasts about 58.6 Earth days, while one year lasts only 88 Earth days. Because the planet has almost no atmosphere, temperatures swing from about minus 173 degrees Celsius at night to 427 degrees Celsius during the day.

Mercury has no moons. Its surface is heavily cratered and resembles the Moon. The largest impact feature is the Caloris Basin, a wide crater created by an ancient collision.
