Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because of iron oxide dust on its surface. It has a diameter of 6,779 kilometers and orbits at an average distance of 227.9 million kilometers.

A day on Mars lasts about 24.6 hours, and a year lasts 687 Earth days. Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. The atmosphere is thin and about 95 percent carbon dioxide. The average surface temperature is about minus 63 degrees Celsius.

Mars has the largest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons, which rises about 21.9 kilometers. It also has a huge canyon system called Valles Marineris. Evidence suggests that liquid water once flowed on the surface.
