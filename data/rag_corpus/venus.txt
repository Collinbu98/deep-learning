Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar in size to Earth. It has a diameter of 12,104 kilometers and orbits at an average distance of 108.2 million kilometers.

Venus rotates very slowly, and its rotation is retrograde, meaning it turns in the opposite direction to most planets. A single day on Venus lasts about 243 Earth days, which is longer than its year of 225 Earth days. Venus therefore has the longest day of any planet.

The atmosphere of Venus is about 96 percent carbon dioxide, with thick clouds of sulfuric acid. Surface pressure is about 92 times the pressure on Earth. A runaway greenhouse effect makes Venus the hottest planet, with a surface temperature of about 465 degrees Celsius. Venus has no moons.
