Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the Solar System. It has a diameter of 139,820 kilometers and orbits at an average distance of 778.5 million kilometers. Jupiter is more massive than all the other planets combined, at about 2.5 times their total mass.

Jupiter rotates very quickly. A day on Jupiter lasts only about 9.9 hours, which is the shortest day of any planet. One year on Jupiter lasts about 11.86 Earth years.

Jupiter is made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Its atmosphere features the Great Red Spot, a giant storm larger than Earth. Jupiter has 95 known moons. The four largest are Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto, known as the Galilean moons.
